## Short description (about 50 words)

Surely Not is a native iPhone app for Sure, the open source finance app you host yourself. See what's still to pay this month, move a transaction to the right category with one tap, check your budget from the lock screen, and send your Apple Card from Wallet straight to your own server.

## Feature blurbs

- **Still to pay.** Bills finds each repeating payment in your transactions itself, so the count is right even when Sure's own record lags. A reminder the day before each one is optional.
- **One-tap categories.** Tap a transaction's category, or press and hold the row, and move it. Filter to one category to see its last six months as a chart.
- **Apple Card in Sure.** Apple has no server API for Apple Card, so the app reads Wallet on your phone and uploads what changed to your Sure server over HTTPS. Edits you made in Sure are never overwritten.
- **Widgets.** What's still due, the next bills, and spending against this month's budget, on the home and lock screen. Amounts are hidden while the phone is locked.
- **Private by default.** Sign-in details live only in the iOS Keychain. Face ID, Touch ID or passcode lock, and figures hidden in the app switcher.
- **Sign in your way.** Single sign-on (Pocket ID, Google and others), email and password with a two-factor code, or an API key.

## Limits to say out loud

- Apple Card, Apple Cash and Savings sync only in the United States; UK connected bank accounts only in the UK. Needs a real iPhone.
- Budgets and most accounts are read-only; change them in Sure on the web.
- Unofficial: not made by, endorsed by or affiliated with the Sure project or Maybe Finance.
